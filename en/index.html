About

The moment you meet Dobit,
we make you shine.

Dobit Salon Beomgye is a hair salon on the 3rd floor of 28 Pyeongchon-daero 223beon-gil, Dongan-gu, Anyang, near Beomgye Station. Opened in 2023, the salon has 15 styling chairs and four designers including the owner.

We offer design cuts (layered, hush, tassel, sleek), women's perms (layered, jelly, hippie, hug, wind, C-curl, S-curl), men's perms (as perm, part perm, shadow perm, down perm), volume straightening and straightening for very curly hair, plus bleach and colors such as ash, matte and red brown.

Every service starts with a one-on-one consultation to suggest a style that balances trends and easy styling. Open all year (closed only on Seollal and Chuseok day): weekdays 11:00–20:00, Saturday 10:00–20:00, Sunday 10:00–19:00. Mechanical parking (small/mid-size cars, 2 hours) is available.

Why Dobit Salon Beomgye

  • Four designers including the owner each focus on their field: layered cuts and perms, men's perms, straightening, and bleach and color.
  • All designers hold the SMAT (service management) certificate and have completed Vidal Sassoon and S3 Academy cut training, L'Oréal, Wella and Milbon color training, and professional perm training.
  • Root color 5-session packages (A, B, C) make regular root touch-ups easier.
  • Healing spa cuts for men and women, plus scalp and head spa care.
