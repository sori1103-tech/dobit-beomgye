FAQ

Haircut FAQ

How much is a haircut?
Junior cut ₩20,000, men's cut ₩22,000, women's cut ₩25,000. Design cuts are ₩22,000 (junior), ₩24,000 (men) and ₩27,000 (women). These are base prices and differ by designer level.
Who should I book for a layered or hush cut?
Layered cuts: Chief Director Dahae. Layered and hush cuts: Owner Sori. Short and mid-length layered cuts and tassel cuts: Senior Team Leader Chaewon. Sleek cuts: Senior Team Leader Joowon.
How much is a healing spa cut?
Men's healing spa cut ₩32,000, women's healing spa cut ₩35,000. We offer healing spa cuts together with scalp and head spa care.
