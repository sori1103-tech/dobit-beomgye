Home/FAQ
How do I book?
Book on Naver or call 031-387-7775. The “Book (Naver)” button at the bottom of the screen opens the booking page.
Can I choose my designer?
Yes. Please choose your designer when booking. See each designer's specialty on the Designers page.
What are your opening hours?
Weekdays 11:00–20:00, Saturday 10:00–20:00, Sunday 10:00–19:00. Open all year; closed only on the day of Seollal and Chuseok.
Is there parking?
Mechanical parking in the building is available for small and mid-size cars for up to 2 hours. When it is full, please use a nearby public or paid lot.
Where are you?
3F #303·304, 28 Pyeongchon-daero 223beon-gil, Dongan-gu, Anyang, near Beomgye Station. See Location.
Do prices differ by designer?
Yes. Prices shown are base prices (S length) and differ by designer level.
Are there extra charges?
Extra length, extra curl or upgraded products may cost more after consultation with your designer. Shampoo is charged separately for partial services (+₩5,000).
Do you have root color packages?
Yes. 5-session root color packages: A ₩200,000, B ₩300,000, C ₩400,000. Root color and root bleach are based on 1–3 cm.
Do you do men's perms?
Yes. As perms, part perms, shadow perms and down perms. Men's basic perm ₩90,000, down perm ₩30,000.
What happens on my first visit?
We start with a one-on-one consultation and suggest a style that balances trends and easy styling before the service.
