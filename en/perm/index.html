Perm & Straightening

Layered, jelly, hippie and hug perms, men's as perms and down perms, and volume straightening.

Home/Perm & Straightening

Women's perms, men's perms and straightening,
each handled by a specialist

Women's perms include layered, jelly, hippie, hug, wind, C-curl and S-curl perms. Men's perms include as perm, part perm, shadow perm and down perm. We also offer volume straightening and straightening for very curly hair.

Layered and hush perms and straightening for very curly hair: Owner Sori. Jelly, hippie, as and part perms: Dahae. Wind and tassel perms and volume straightening: Chaewon. Hug perms, layered C/S-curl perms and down perms: Joowon. Changing color too? See Color & Bleach.

Recommended for

  • Adding volume and waves to a layered cut (layered perm, hush perm)
  • Trendy waves such as jelly, hippie or hug perms
  • Men whose side hair sticks out (as perm, part perm, down perm)
  • Anyone tired of styling curly hair every day (volume straightening)

FAQ

Perm FAQ

Do you do men's perms?
Yes. We offer as perms, part perms, shadow perms and down perms. Men's basic perm is ₩90,000 and down perm is ₩30,000. Dahae specializes in as and part perms; Joowon in as perms and down perms.
How much is volume straightening?
Volume straightening (S) ₩170,000, root straightening ₩100,000, straightening + setting perm (S) ₩220,000. These are base prices for S length; extra length, extra curl or upgraded products may cost more after consultation.
Who should I book for a layered perm?
Owner Sori is a layered-perm specialist and does Elizabeth perms, hush layered perms, build perms and tassel perms after a one-on-one consultation. Joowon specializes in layered C/S-curl perms and Chaewon in wind and tassel perms.
