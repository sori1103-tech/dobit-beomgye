We offer bleach, colors such as ash, matte and red brown, and partial or full design color. Root color 5-session packages (A, B, C) make regular touch-ups easier.

Bleach: Chief Director Dahae. Ash brown: Chaewon. Custom color: Owner Sori. For hair care after coloring, see Treatment & Scalp Care.

Recommended for

  • Regular root touch-ups (5-session packages)
  • Anyone who wants a specific color after bleaching
  • Trend colors such as ash, matte or red brown
  • Men who want a basic color with their cut

FAQ

Color FAQ

What are the root color packages?
5-session root color passes: Package A ₩200,000, Package B ₩300,000, Package C ₩400,000. Single sessions are Root Color A ₩50,000, B ₩70,000 and C ₩100,000.
How much regrowth does root color cover?
Root color and root bleach are based on 1–3 cm of regrowth. Shampoo is charged separately for partial services (+₩5,000).
How much is bleach?
Bleach A (S) ₩120,000, Bleach B (S) ₩140,000, root bleach ₩70,000. These are base prices for S length; extra length or upgraded products may cost more. Chief Director Dahae specializes in bleach.
